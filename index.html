What is En Kanavu En Ethirkaalam?

“En Kanavu, En Ethirkaalam” is a visionary initiative focused on understanding how the Government of Tamil Nadu can meaningfully support the youth in achieving their dreams. This statewide survey collects insights from young people across every district and town, helping the government identify their real needs, challenges, and aspirations. Whether it is access to essential tools, modern gadgets, skill-development programs, professional training, apprenticeships, job opportunities, online courses, AI subscriptions, musical instruments, or the chance to meet and learn from industry experts — every dream matters. Through this platform, the Government aims to empower youth by mapping both their immediate and long-term goals up to the year 2030. By listening to their voices and understanding the obstacles they face, the initiative strives to create a supportive ecosystem where every young person in Tamil Nadu can rise, progress, and fulfil their true potential.”

M.K Stalin

Hon'ble Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu
